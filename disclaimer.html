BloxVault's tools produce estimates, calculations, and information read from files you provide. They are meant to help with planning, not to replace your own judgment, your slicer, or your printer's documentation.

Before you rely on a result

Independently verify anything a tool on this site tells you, including:

  • Dimensions and measurements
  • Material properties, including filament density and diameter
  • Cost estimates, including material, electricity, labor, and fee figures you enter yourself
  • Printer compatibility and build volume
  • Slicer settings and any G-code produced by a slicer
  • Machine settings, including temperatures and speeds
  • Mechanical and electrical safety of your printer and its setup

Specific tools

Will it fit? compares overall bounding-box dimensions only. It does not check orientation, supports, or whether a specific shape will actually print well at a given scale.

Print cost and pricing uses the numbers you enter in your saved setup. It cannot know your actual material cost, actual electricity rate, or actual failure rate; the result is only as accurate as those inputs.

G-code reader reads summary comment lines that slicers write into a file. Not every slicer or version writes the same comments, so a value it cannot find is shown as not found rather than guessed. Values calculated from filament length use typical density figures, not a measurement of your actual filament.
