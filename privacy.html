Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026. This describes what the BloxVault site actually does, based on its current code. It is not legal advice and is not a claim of compliance with any specific law.

The tools (Fit, Spool, Cost, G-code reader)

These four tools run entirely in your browser. No account is required to use them, and the numbers you type in are not sent to a server.

Your printer and filament setup (printer name, bed size, wattage, filament name, price, electricity rate, and similar figures) is saved only in your browser's local storage, on your own device. BloxVault does not receive, store, or have access to this information. Clearing your browser's site data, or using a different browser or device, removes it.

On the G-code reader, the file you choose is read directly in your browser using your browser's file-reading feature. It is never uploaded or transmitted anywhere.

The optional account

The account page is optional and is not required to use any tool. If you register, BloxVault uses Firebase Authentication and Firebase's Firestore database, both operated by Google, to handle sign-in and to store your profile.

If you create an account, the following is stored in Firestore: the email address you sign up with, an optional display name you choose, whether you signed up with email/password or Google, and the dates you joined and last logged in. BloxVault does not collect payment information, physical addresses, or phone numbers through this site.

Access to this data is restricted by Firestore security rules: an account can read and write only its own profile, and only specific fields.

Cookies and local storage

BloxVault's own code does not set tracking or advertising cookies. If you use the optional account, the Firebase Authentication SDK stores session information in your browser (through mechanisms such as local storage or IndexedDB) so that you stay signed in; this is part of Firebase's standard behavior and is not configured or read by BloxVault directly.

Third-party services

Pages on this site load two external resources from third-party providers over the network each time you visit:

  • Bootstrap's stylesheet, from jsDelivr's content delivery network
  • On the account page only, the Firebase SDK, from Google's gstatic.com

Loading these means your browser makes a request to those providers' servers, which may log standard web request information (such as your IP address) under their own privacy practices. BloxVault does not control, and makes no claims about, what jsDelivr or Google do with that request data. No advertising or analytics service is used on this site.

Data retention

Your local browser setup and the account profile described above are kept until you remove them. Account data is kept for as long as the account exists.

Data deletion

You can clear your saved setup at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser. BloxVault does not currently have a self-service "delete my account" button. To request deletion of an account and its profile data, contact BloxVault using the details on the Contact page.

Security

Account data is protected by Firestore security rules that deny access by default and allow an account to read or write only its own profile. No system is guaranteed to be free of vulnerabilities, and BloxVault makes no claim of guaranteed security.

Children's privacy

BloxVault does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The site does not verify age and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site changes. Continued use of the site after an update means you accept the current version.
