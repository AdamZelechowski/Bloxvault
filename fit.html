How it works

The tool multiplies each side of the model by the scale and compares it with your bed size, also trying the model turned 90° on the bed. It only compares overall width, depth, and height, so it can't tell whether a shape will actually print well in that position.

Bigger prints use much more filament and time than you might expect. Solid prints grow with the cube of the scale, and thin-walled ones closer to the square, so the tool shows a range instead of one number.
