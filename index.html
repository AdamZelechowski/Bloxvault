3D printing toolbox

Simple tools that remember your printer and filament, so you set them once and never retype them.

New to 3D printing? Read this first
Slicer
Free software that turns a 3D model into instructions for your printer. After slicing, it shows the print time and how many grams of filament the print will use. Most tools here ask for those two numbers.
Filament
The plastic on the spool that the printer melts to build the object. It's sold by weight, usually 1 kg per spool.
Bed
The flat surface the print sits on. Its size limits how big one print can be.
Failed print
A print that goes wrong and gets thrown away. It happens to everyone, so the cost tool adds a small allowance for it.
My setupEdit

Saved in this browser. Starting values are placeholders, so change them to match your printer and filament.
