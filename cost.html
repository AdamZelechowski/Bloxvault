Cost breakdown

How it's calculated

Filament cost is the weight used times your price per kilogram. Electricity is your printer's average power draw times the print time times your electricity price.

The failed-print allowance spreads the cost of reprints over every good print: at a 10% failure rate, each good print carries about an 11% extra share of its filament and electricity. Your time is charged at your hourly rate.

The suggested price is set so that after marketplace fees are taken out, you keep your chosen profit share of the sale price. If fees plus profit reach 100%, no price can satisfy both, so the calculator asks you to lower one.
